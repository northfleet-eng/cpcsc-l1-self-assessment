## What this repository is not

CPCSC Level 1 is one tier of a three-tier program. This repository covers Level 1 only.

- **Level 1** — self-attestation against 13 controls. Effective April 1, 2026.
- **Level 2** — third-party assessment by a Standards Council of Canada accredited certification body. Scheduled for spring 2027. Maps to the full ITSP.10.171 control set (approximately 97 controls across 17 families).
- **Level 3** — Government of Canada assessment by the Department of National Defence. Reserved for the highest-risk scenarios, weapon systems, critical infrastructure, and sensitive Five Eyes information.

For Levels 2 and 3, refer to the PSPC and CCCS source documents above.

CPCSC Level 1 also covers "specified information" only. This is the unclassified federal contractual information held by a supplier. Controlled Information triggers Level 2 or higher and the full ITSP.10.171 surface. Protected B and higher classifications fall outside CPCSC entirely and are governed by separate accreditation regimes (ITSG-33, CCCS Medium Cloud Profile, etc.).
