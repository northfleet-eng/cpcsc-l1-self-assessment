## NIST SP 800-171A Rev. 3 lineage

CPCSC Level 1 is based on the Canadian version of NIST SP 800-171A Rev. 3 *Assessing Security Requirements for Controlled Unclassified Information*. There are no substantial technical changes between the Canadian document and the NIST source; differences reflect the Canadian regulatory, policy, directive, standards, and guidelines landscape only.

The "Source assessment procedures" listed for each control in this repository (for example, AC-02, AC-03, AC-20, AC-22, IA-02, IA-03, IA-11, MP-06, PE-02, PE-03, PE-05, SC-07, SI-02, SI-03) trace back to the NIST source procedures and remain valid cross-reference identifiers for organizations whose evidence inventory is already mapped against NIST SP 800-171A Rev. 3 or NIST SP 800-53.

- **NIST SP 800-171A Rev. 3 (NIST publication):** <https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/171/a/r3/final>

## What this repository is not

CPCSC Level 1 is one tier of a three-tier program. This repository covers Level 1 only. As PSPC's April 2026 backgrounder describes the levels:

- **Level 1:** an annual self-assessment against 13 controls. Available to suppliers since April 1, 2026, and added to select defence contracts from summer 2026, required at contract award.
- **Level 2:** an external assessment every three years by a certification body accredited through the Standards Council of Canada. To be added to select defence contracts from spring 2027, for contracts that involve controlled defence information or more complex cyber-sensitive work.
- **Level 3:** an assessment every three years conducted by National Defence. Reserved for the highest-risk work: weapon systems, critical infrastructure access, or sensitive information shared with Five Eyes partners.

For Levels 2 and 3, refer to the PSPC and CCCS source documents above.

CPCSC protects specified information: sensitive, non-classified government information that must be protected when non-Government of Canada organizations handle, process, or store it. PSPC's examples include unclassified contract details not intended for public release, controlled goods information, and protected information. Classified information is outside CPCSC, which covers information below the classified level.
